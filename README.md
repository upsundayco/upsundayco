## We are UpSunday

A San Diego studio for brand, web and motion design. We design and build identities, websites and motion for founders and teams who want their brand to look as good as the work behind it.

Strategy, design and engineering sit in one team, so nothing gets lost between the deck and the launch. Every site we ship is fast on every screen, built to be found by search and answer engines, and easy for your team to run after we hand over the keys.

## How we work

| | Step | What happens |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **01** | **Discover** | A call to understand the business, the audience and what the brand has to do. |
| **02** | **Design** | Direction, then every page designed in Figma and reviewed with you before a line of code. |
| **03** | **Build** | Engineered in house, previewed on a live link as it comes together. |
| **04** | **Launch** | Performance, accessibility and search checks, then a handover your team can run with. |
