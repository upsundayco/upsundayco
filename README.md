## We are UpSunday

A San Diego studio for brand, web and motion design. We design and build websites, identities and motion for founders and teams who want their brand to look as good as the work behind it.

Every site we ship is designed in house and engineered to last: fast on every screen, built to be found by search and answer engines, and easy for your team to run after launch.

Great design doesn't wait for Monday.

## What we do

| Discipline | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Brand** | identity systems, logos, type and color, brand guidelines |
| **Web** | marketing sites, ecommerce, client portals, custom CMS |
| **Motion** | site animation, 3D, launch films and social motion |

## How we build

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, GSAP, Three.js and Blender, deployed on Vercel.
